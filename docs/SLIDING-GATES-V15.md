# Full-width barred cell fronts · v0.15.0

All 48 cell fronts now have fixed steel bars across their room width. A smaller 1.4m door slides laterally over a fixed side panel on an overhead track; it does not swing into the room or lift into the ceiling. Upper doors are 2.8m high, lower doors 2.6m high. Full-height side panels and barred transoms complete each front. Room widths/depths and both floors are retained. The corridor is reduced from 22m to 18m at the user's request: two 3m upper galleries border a 12m opening. The southern row moves 4m closer; stairs center on Z=7. Guards, medical fixtures, floors, lights and the map follow the reduced layout. Versioned checkpoints migrate cells/galleries by 4m and retain supported stair/lower-hall positions.

Each leaf travels 1.5m in 0.7 seconds. The parking side is selected to stay within its own cell frontage. The leaf occupies a separate depth lane to avoid overlapping/z-fighting with fixed bars. Collision follows its actual translated bounds each frame. An occupied sweep pauses for the player or an officer; opening flags alone never remove the physical leaf's collision. Fixed grilles block movement and transmit sight/audio like bars. Checkpoint restoration snaps to the saved open/closed state. Cell 46 remains held open for exploration in the current chapter, including older saves. Other latches and legacy story prerequisites remain.

The opening cinematic uses the same sliding pose for Cell 47, keeping its escape/shot/lock/inspection timing. The key-through-bars exception remains limited to the reachable Cell 47 key. No new third-party assets or licenses are introduced; existing metal PBR and shared geometry/material batches are reused. Guard AI and navigation rules retain their authority over animation.
