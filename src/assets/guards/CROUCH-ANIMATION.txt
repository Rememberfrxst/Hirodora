Guard Crouching Idle
====================

Source: user-supplied `Crouching Idle.fbx`, downloaded from Adobe Mixamo after
uploading the Hirodora guard character.

Runtime derivative: `crouch-inspect.glb`

Changes: removed duplicate character geometry, textures, lights and camera;
retained the 65-bone Mixamo source motion at 76 frames / 30 FPS. At load time the
motion is retargeted once, with leg-length scaling, onto the existing 69-bone
Hirodora officer rest rig. All officer LODs reuse that converted clip and their
existing meshes/materials.

The supplied file is treated as user-authorized project input. Adobe Mixamo
content usage remains subject to the user's Adobe account and Mixamo terms.
